# Privacy

The 授業アシスタント plugin itself collects, stores, and sends no data.

- It reads and writes files in the teacher's own Google Drive, Sheets, Docs, Slides, and Classroom through Google's official connectors, after the teacher signs in from Claude's connector settings. Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Where no official connector exists (Google Forms, NotebookLM, or Classroom when its connector is not set up), it operates those pages in the teacher's own browser with Claude in Chrome.
- Student names, email addresses, and grades are read only when needed and are never written into the plugin's own files.
- It never posts, sends, publishes, or finalizes grades without the teacher's confirmation.
- Anthropic's privacy policy applies to Claude itself: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
